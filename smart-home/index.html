What the app does

Local control

On the same Wi-Fi network, commands go straight to the device even when the internet is down.

Remote control

Away from home, control the same devices over the internet.

Schedules and scenes

Switch devices at set times, or trigger a group of devices with one tap.

Family sharing

Share your home or a room with family members on their own accounts.

Supported devices

The app works with control units installed by Volt Pro, built on ESP32 microcontrollers. Devices are added from inside the app over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi.
